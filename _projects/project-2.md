summary: >-
  This project is to innovate AI-driven techniques for modelling the complex interactions between heat exposure and human mortality, considering a full range of socio-economic determinants of weather exposure and health outcomes. This project is a collabration with Dr Eunice Lo, and funded by the Faculty of Science and Engineering's Strategic Research Accelerator programme. 
---

In this project, We develop spatially explicit machine learning and knowledge graphs to integrate high-resolution temperature, humidity, air pollution, mortality, demographic, socio-economic, urbanisation, housing, transport, greenspace, and access to healthcare data in the UK. This AI-powered integration will enable us to build a never-before-seen model to predict heat-related mortality, and to downscale this model to ultra-high spatial resolution for heat adaptation, and urban planning, thereby benefiting society. Our UK model allows us to infer new knowledge to other regions in subsequent research, e.g., the Global South, where critical heat-health data and models are lacking but are essential for adapting vulnerable populations to global warming.
